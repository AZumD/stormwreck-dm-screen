The generic entries in `js/catalogue-seeds/compendium-bestiary.js`, `js/catalogue-seeds/compendium-bestiary-ii.js`, and `js/catalogue-seeds/compendium-items.js` are condensed and reformatted for this project's table-reference UI. They are not intended to preserve source layout or explanatory prose verbatim. Entries identify their source as `SRD 5.1 (CC BY 4.0)` so the provenance remains visible inside the Compendium.

The item library includes SRD weapon and armor statistics, adventuring equipment, consumables, and a curated magic-item reference set. The bestiary includes SRD creature statistics and condensed table-facing descriptions of traits and actions.

Stormwreck-specific material, user-authored campaign content, and summaries of playable options from non-SRD books are not presented as SRD material.
